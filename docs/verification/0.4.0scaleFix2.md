## Calibration limits
- Hypothetical equal 50% allocations and one grass target per side, F=400 from m=1, v=10m/s, dt=0.025s idealized stop: `Q=64.078`. At `H=24,T=32,D=25,E=22,damageGain=2.6`, threshold is expected to be reached in a *pure damage preview*. Actual Rapier force can differ materially. F=200 produces a lower Q and no one-hit grass threshold in the same synthetic setup.
- F=40,000 yields Q=6,439.678, remaining distinct from Q(F=400); cap is reached around F=407,058. Direct damage per block still saturates at 255 and large-scale collision realism is unverified.
- Material sheet is a reference dataset; the current runtime explicit anchors include grass/reinforced deepslate but **do not automatically import the entire CSV**. No claim of full 0.4.1 sheet application.
- R2 runtime verification reads at most 256 unique owner-cells/side (moreFix deviation from 32), while approved damage targets are at most 32/side. Exceeding the hard scan limit quarantines that side.
