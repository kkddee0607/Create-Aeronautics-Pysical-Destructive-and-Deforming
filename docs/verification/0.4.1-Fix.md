## Changes and acceptance contracts

1. **Five-by-five contact area, four-corner bias** — `ContactFacePatch` reconstructs one axis-aligned, co-planar, bounded contact face from at least four independent native witness records spanning its corners, with consistent normals and a known Plot-grid offset. `SableContactRegions.audit` reuses `OwnerResolver.NativeAuditBatch.inspect` to individually verify a real occupied owner cell and same-plane collision shape for each proposed target, and only then adds a distinct target to the original episode's single credit. Evidence-free, rotated/oblique, offset-inconsistent or over-budget faces fall back to original native verified seeds. MAX_AREA=10,000 candidate cells. The shadow/World pipeline is unchanged. **The test is synthetic** and does not prove four-corner recovery for every in-game orientation or exact two-body overlap on all candidate cells.
2. **Higher limit and saturating source F** — `GameImpactCredit.MAX_GAME_F = (100³) × (4 mass units) × (10 speed units) = 40,000,000` (unit convention is explicitly hypothetical; actual contact duration is unknown). Valid observations above that are clamped to F=40,000,000 rather than treated as invalid. With `forceGain=0.161`, `forceFloor=2`, the default derived `maxGroupQ = 6,439,999.678`. Existing property `-Dpdd.realfix.maxGroupQ` can still intentionally reduce the cap; no backdated source credits are recalculated. Unknown ownership, unverified first contact, failed rearm, invalid source records, or insufficient geometry remain fail-closed. R2 runtime audit limit 32,768 unique cells per side, region-target cap 16,384 per side, F propagation graph cap 32,768, World queue cap 16,384, preview-only state cap 16,384, per-tick mutation work cap unchanged (64 default). Sable's native 100-record cap and `WorldClearanceVerifier` maximum scanning proof are **not lifted**; giant plots may still be unverifiable under that separate safety contract.
3. **Directional propagation shape** — verified-direction `ForcePropagation` cone now begins with the actual **verified seed footprint**, narrows inward; maximum geometric depth bounded at 32 and grows monotonically (logarithmically) with allotted F, and propagation may stop earlier due to damping/threshold/unknown neighbors. Unknown direction falls back to original unbiased traversal. No extra force or Q credited.
4. **World material eligibility** — `NeoForgeWorldGateway` no longer uses a hardcoded vanilla block allow-list or requires an entire collision cube/analog-output exclusion; netherite, slime, and ordinary modded single-cell blocks are eligible. Existing protections remain: loaded chunk, World vs Plot ownership, state/owner generation/incarnation CAS, explicit `unbreakable` flag and negative destroy speed, block entities, vanilla multi-cell door/bed/double-plant cases, fake player permission and BreakEvent veto, Vanilla `destroyBlock` with one drop path and WAL. Generic modded linked-multiblock transactions are not automatically inferred; unknown/unsafe external machines may still require an owner-specific adapter. The user request “every World block” is **limited by these mandatory safety contracts** rather than silently permitting destructive corruption.
