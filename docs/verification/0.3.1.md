### What is specifically NOT claimed

- Sable native `local_p1/p2` is collider-local in pinned source; the consumer-grid/post-effect positional mapping is **not** a complete validated collider pose in all rotation cases. No exact solver manifold, unique native contact ID, impulse or joules are claimed.
- Local `risingRatio` and `quietFrames` settings exist and the rising-edge condition is independently tested, but force-only rotation does not generate additional credits: independent *contact-region geometry* must be verified to activate that pathway. Unknown contact becomes Q=0, not fabricated evidence.
- Many distinct simultaneously contacted blocks can share **one event Q**, not independent `Q=64` per block. Multiple *truly independent* force events in the same pair require proof of separate cause and episode; this version does not invent such solver events.
- No block state, NBT, damage, deform, drops or collider rebuild is performed. Those stages are owned by 0.4.x–0.8.x.

## 7. Two-session in-game procedure and acceptance criteria

### Session A: static World and reimpact

- Before test: enable PDD 0.3.1; verify `[PDD-DIAG] INIT version=0.3.1`, `[PDD-0.3.1] phase=START`; preserve the four sample assets. A1 static stone wall; A2 static grass ground; B1 rope-linked mace Plot (levitite handle, reinforced-deepslate/chain weight). Test only after Gradle/Callback workflow pass.
- **A1**: Move B1 from a visibly clear, non-touching position, pause for two actual physics frames then hit A1. Observe `PDD-NATIVE-TARGET`, `PDD-EPISODE`, `[PDD-LEDGER]` and the block coordinates; one newly authorized grouped Q maximum 64; no second credit from contact records of the same impact; no unrelated world block selected.
- **A2**: Drop B1 on A2 and leave it touching/sliding for **at least 20 server ticks**. Source force records may continue, but no additional `ISSUED` for an unseparated contact episode. Lift B1 clear for at least **two confirmed physics frames** (not 2 wall-clock seconds), then strike again; expect a new `episodeGeneration` and one additional granted Q, if geometry and targets verify. If targets are unverified, `Q=0` with reason is allowed rather than fabricated approval.
- Validate each `PDD-LEDGER`: `Q<=64`, `allocatedA+allocatedB+unused=Q`; no more than four distinct target blocks on one side; `mutation=OFF`.

### Session B: multi-region Plot/Plot and B3

- B2: balloon Plot with envelope, burner, chain/weight. B3: independent movable stone-wall Plot. Confirm B1, B2, B3 have **different stable Plot UUIDs** before impact.
- Strike B1→B2 and B1→B3, including corner/rotated contacts. Inspect per-owner verified target coordinates, `regions` and `aTargets/bTargets` in `[PDD-LEDGER]`. Two distinct verified contact blocks on the same side should share its **one side budget**; reported contact points cannot multiply group Q.
- Separate B1 and B3 physically; re-approach, strike twice, wait under sustained contact, then separate again. Check `[PDD-EPISODE] phaseBefore=... authorizationTick=... episodeGeneration=...`. If an `ACTIVE`→`APPROVED` occurs (old log tick 86305 pattern), the episode generation and authorization tick must demonstrate a prior verified ARMED approach in that two-frame burst. Otherwise reject it as a potential duplicate-credit defect.
- Verify no Sable original callback side effects change; if a direct registered special block is used, preserve callback invocation count and original return semantics. A plain stone/chain sample may not fire a direct callback: absence alone is not a defect.

**FAIL:** unverified block receives Q, group Q is multiplied by record/region count, Q duplicated while continuously touching, owner epoch reused on split/reassembly, original Sable callback result altered, unexpected world mutation, crash or bridge exception.

**Submit on failure:** full `latest.log` from game start, matching SableBridge logs (if relevant), complete `[PDD-OBS]`, `[PDD-NATIVE-TARGET]`, `[PDD-TARGET]`, `[PDD-EPISODE]`, `[PDD-LEDGER]`, `[PDD-0.3.1]` lines around failing tick, precise scene and block/Plot UUIDs, plus Actions Build/Callback logs for compilation/test failures.
