## Root cause and correction
- 0.4.0Fix `SableContactRegions` treated **unique owner-cell shape reads** and **distinct Q damage targets** as if both could use a shared limit of 32 per owner in runtime. An otherwise verified collision could become `SIDE_A_INCOMPLETE` once 32 unique cells were inspected, even when far fewer than 32 targets had been confirmed.
- This release separates *runtime R2 verification-cell budget* (at most **256 new owner-cells per side per authorized event**) from *ledger damage-target limit* (unchanged at **32 distinct owner-local targets per side**). The pure/test `collect()` entrypoint retains the legacy 32-cell policy; the real `audit()` invokes `collectWithBudget(...,256)`. The per-owner/per-cell inspection cache remains in force. Native record limit of 100 and Q allocation are unchanged.
- The change is deliberately bounded and conservative. Large manifolds may still exhaust 256 unique cells; the exhausted side stays **quarantined** with no Q, the other independently verified side may receive a `PARTIAL` credit, and target oversubscription still quarantines that side. This is a targeted mitigation with strict resource ceilings, **not proof that every possible manifold is completely scanned**.
- `worldMutation=OFF`; original Sable callback semantics, ledger once-per-episode, owner space and fingerprint attribution, damage preview and persistence retained.
- The preview checkpoint remains `pdd-0.4.0Fix-preview-only.bin` to restore historical 0.4.0Fix shadow data; it remains non-authoritative, does not break blocks, and cannot be used as actual world damage.
