## Additional safety findings from the source audit

1. **Premature damage invalidation (fixed):** The `LevelChunk#setBlockState` mixin ran at `HEAD` and erased saved damage even for no-op/failed block state changes. The mixin now checks `CallbackInfoReturnable#getReturnValue()` at `RETURN`; no-op (`null`) changes do not discard damage. It still invalidates after successful changes, including a changed state with the same fingerprint following an intervening replacement. Full Minecraft/mixin-runtime verification remains required.
2. **Spurious break event during damage-only accounting (fixed):** `NeoForgeWorldGateway.canDamage()` posted `BlockEvent.BreakEvent` for damage-only plans. That could trigger handlers as though a block were broken before it was. `canDamage` now verifies loaded state, fake-player permissions and block safety without a break event. `canBreak` posts one cancellable BreakEvent for a real threshold-reaching break and performs a fresh snapshot check before the Vanilla `destroyBlock` call. Claims that do not participate in `mayInteract`/BreakEvent require in-game compatibility validation.
3. **Unbounded watched-position growth (fixed):** The world service retained every watched cell for its entire runtime, even after the damage was cleared and all intents completed. It now tracks bounded pending intent counts and releases idle, non-quarantined, undamaged cell watches. No historical World scan was added.
4. **WAL write pressure (bounded):** The core previously allowed up to 512 or more synchronous receipt operations per tick by default (64 break cap × 8). This patch also bounds total per-tick operations to 64 by default (or the configured higher break cap). Queued work is retained, not dropped, and the original 64-break-per-tick ceiling remains.
5. **Misleading summary (fixed):** The top-level 100-tick status log printed `worldMutation=OFF` unconditionally. It now reflects whether WorldCommitService is active. Existing read-only F/Q and diagnostic paths are unchanged.
